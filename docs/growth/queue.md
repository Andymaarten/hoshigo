# Outreach queue

Drafted by Sora, checked 28 September 2026. **Andreas sends everything himself**, from his own address or account, after reading and changing the draft so it's his. Nothing here is sent automatically.

How to use it: send at most three a week, on different days (see `strategy.md`). Change the status to `sent YYYY-MM-DD` and write what happens in `log.md`. One gentle follow-up at most, after two weeks, and only if the first message invited a reply.

Before sending, check the "verify" notes: a few contact details were only readable indirectly.

Ranked by how promising I think they are. Nine places made the cut. Several others were considered and left out; they are listed at the bottom, with reasons.

## 5. Show HN (Hacker News)

- **Link:** https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html
- **What it is:** the Hacker News section for things people made. Large, international, critical and technical readers.
- **Size and activity:** very active daily.
- **Rules found** (showhn.html): "Show HN is for something you've made that other people can play with", from someone "around to discuss". Off topic: "sign-up pages, newsletters, lists". "Make it easy for users to try your thing out, ideally without barriers such as signups". "Don't post quickly-generated one-offs".
- **Why hoshigo fits:** it's a real, working product, personally made, with a strong point of view. **Caveat:** making a page needs a signup. Link to a public page (Andreas's) so people can look without an account, and say plainly that making your own needs an email. If that feels like a stretch of the rule, wait until there's something to try without signing up.
- **Best channel:** Andreas posts it himself from his own long-standing HN account (not a new one), in week 4 at the earliest, on a weekday morning US Eastern time, and stays to answer comments that day.
- **Status:** to send (week 4, only if strangers can make a page unaided)

Title: `Show HN: hoshigo – a personal page for only the things you'd give five stars`

(The en dash is Hacker News convention; this isn't hoshigo site copy.)

> I made hoshigo with my partner Saar. It's a small page for the handful of films, books, albums, essays and places you'd give five stars, and a way to see your friends' pages. No feed, no ads, no algorithm, no AI. We'd honestly like people to be gone within minutes.
>
> You can look at a page without an account, for example mine: https://hoshigo.cc/andymaarten. Making your own needs an email address (magic link, no password).
>
> Built with Next.js and Supabase. Covers come from open catalogues (MusicBrainz, Open Library, TMDB). Listings link out to wherever the thing lives. Non-friends see the latest five per list; friends see everything.
>
> I'd love critique, especially on whether the "only five stars" constraint makes sense to you, and what would make you keep one.
